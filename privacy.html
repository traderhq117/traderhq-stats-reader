Privacy Policy

TraderHQ Stats Reader reads only the account owner's own TikTok profile and video statistics, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Display API with the owner's authorization.

The app does not sell, share, or transfer personal data to any third party.

No data is stored on a server beyond what is needed to display the owner's own statistics to the owner.

Questions about this app can be sent to the account owner via their TikTok account traderhq.
